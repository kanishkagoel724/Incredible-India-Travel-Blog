Traveler's Guide to India

Agra

Visit the iconic Taj Mahal early in the morning to avoid crowds. Don't miss exploring Agra Fort and tasting the local delicacy, Petha.

Bangalore

Explore the vibrant city life at MG Road, visit Lalbagh Botanical Garden, and experience the local South Indian cuisine.

Chandigarh

Stroll through the Rock Garden and Sukhna Lake. Enjoy the well-planned city's cleanliness and modern architecture.

Dehradun

Explore Robber's Cave and enjoy the serene environment of Sahastradhara. Visit the Forest Research Institute for a touch of history.

Delhi

Visit historical landmarks like Red Fort, India Gate, and Qutub Minar. Enjoy street food at Chandni Chowk and shop at Connaught Place.

Jaipur

Discover the Pink City's charm with visits to Hawa Mahal, Amber Fort, and City Palace. Don't forget to shop for local handicrafts.

Mumbai

Explore Marine Drive, Gateway of India, and Elephanta Caves. Savor street food at Juhu Beach and experience the bustling city life.

Nainital

Enjoy boating on Naini Lake, visit Naina Devi Temple, and take a cable car ride to Snow View Point for stunning views.
